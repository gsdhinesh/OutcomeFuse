- (change) Architecture-driven amendment, applied 2026-09-07 while distilling the architecture spine at planning-artifacts/architecture/architecture-OutcomeFuse-2026-09-07. Two divergences between the PRD and the architecture were closed rather than left to drift. (1) Section 4.3 cut order gained a new position 2, the third host adapter. Three host frameworks are committed - LangGraph, Microsoft Agent Framework, OpenAI Agents SDK - where FR51 requires only two dissimilar ones; the third was committed with no declared way to drop it, which is exactly the failure mode the cut order exists to prevent. It sits at position 2 because a third adapter multiplies the benchmark matrix (wrapper, conformance passage, four workloads of tools rebound, a frozen FR64 baseline, a share of FR100 failure cases) without adding an argument FR51 does not already have at two. Positions 2 through 10 shifted to 3 through 11 and the Appendix B cut-position labels were corrected accordingly: F8 5 to 6, F7 6 to 7, F9 7 to 8, F15 9 to 10. F14 remains position 1. (2) FR107 added inside F11 - the adapter-conformance battery. The architecture makes verdict enforcement COOPERATIVE: the governor decides, the adapter applies. That means an adapter which executes a gated or denied call while recording a clean pause produces a plausible, internally consistent and entirely false audit trail, and every claim in section 8 then rests on it. A decision record cannot detect the one failure it is the evidence for, so FR107 requires the battery to assert against the decision record AND to carry an out-of-band side-effect probe in which scripted tools assert for themselves whether they were invoked. This work was previously in no feature and no estimate. Appendix B header updated to FR1-FR107 and the claim-guard table gained a row for it. FR ids remain stable; FR107 is placed inside F11 rather than appended, per the document's own rule.
- (change) Second architecture-driven amendment, 2026-09-09, closing the two carried architecture open questions. FR set now FR1-FR109; ids stable, both additions placed inside their feature rather than appended. (1) FR108 added to F2 - before the FR65 freeze one Outcome Contract must be authored per committed workload and every intended criterion classified existing-verifier, new-deterministic-verifier-required, or advisory, with the review explicitly covering the semantic criteria that are easiest to miss: root-cause correctness, query-result correctness, code-location correctness, evidence completeness, and whether a conclusion is supported rather than merely present. At most three additional deterministic verifier types may be admitted, each reusable, pure, bounded, declarative, project-owned, I/O-free and fully tested; semantic matching, model-judged quality and citation-supports-claim are barred unless reducible to deterministic comparison against frozen structured reference data. The rationale is the asymmetric failure mode - FR8 already makes an unverifiable criterion advisory, so a registry shortfall never breaks anything, it quietly shrinks the floor until the gate checks presence and type and section 8.2a's honest limit becomes the ordinary case. (2) FR65 extended - the freeze artifact must now carry the verifier-registry version and hash plus per-workload counts and percentages of mandatory criteria that are reference-backed versus constraint-backed, every mandatory criterion must resolve to a registered deterministic verifier at freeze time, advisory criteria must be listed with their reason, and a workload whose constraint-backed mandatory count exceeds its reference-backed count must be reported as predominantly constraint-backed wherever its results are claimed. The registry joins the freeze because a criterion takes its executable meaning from a verifier type, so a frozen rubric on an unfrozen registry is not frozen. (3) NFR12 replaced with a two-tier retention profile mvp-synthetic-v1 - raw synthetic evidence 60 calendar days from a harness-owned run_closed_at then whole-directory deletion, redacted decision records and proof artifacts 180 calendar days from campaign seal, no ad hoc extension (expired evidence is regenerated by re-running the frozen case), content-free deletion receipts written to a separate append-only manifest and never appended after a sealed terminal row, and an explicit access matrix in which the driver writes only its own run, the harness is sole programmatic reader, the blind-review renderer holds evidence plus contract but no verdict or manifest, and verifiers, host adapters, the static viewer, the submission generator and the model and approval adapters have no access at all. (4) FR109 added to F10 - the runtime refuses to persist non-synthetic evidence until a separate production-data governance profile is approved, rather than applying the MVP profile to it; the profile must define storage location, tenant isolation, encryption, identities, RBAC, legal classification, retention by data class, deletion SLA and verification, backup and replica deletion, incident handling, audit access, export restrictions, and whether specific raw fields may be persisted at all. Refusing is the only behaviour that makes the governance crossing visible, because the alternative failure needs nobody to decide anything. Appendix B updated: header to FR1-FR109, F2 and F10 rows, and a new claim-guard row for the substantive-versus-structural floor.
