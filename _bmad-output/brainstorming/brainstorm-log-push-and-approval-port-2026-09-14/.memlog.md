- (technique) started First Principles Thinking
- (direction) Destination the user is holding: the library emits logs and approval actions generically; every experience (chat, UI) is built OUTSIDE the library by reading logs and answering approvals.
- (insight) Bedrock fact established: the wait is real. Something in the library genuinely has to sit and wait for an unspecified span of time before the gated call can proceed.
- (idea) Wait is minutes-scale and configurable. If nobody responds within it, abort the session rather than proceeding. Bounded wait, terminate on silence.
- (direction) Session parked mid-First-Principles to switch to bmad-prd update. Unresolved and worth resuming: the asymmetry question - log is high-frequency, one-way, nobody need listen, silence costs nothing; approval is rare, blocking, must be heard, silence is fatal. They may not want to be one mechanism. Provocation and What If techniques never ran.
- (decision) Closed early at the user's request to move to architecture. First Principles produced the finding that mattered - stream and approval are two mechanisms, not one - and it is already carried into the PRD as F17/F18. Provocation and What If were never run.
